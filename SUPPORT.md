## Questions and feedback

Search existing issues first. Include the relevant page, version or commit, reproduction steps, and expected and actual results. For data issues, include the platform, public problem or contest link, and time range. Remove sensitive information from logs and screenshots.

Submit vulnerabilities through [private reporting](https://github.com/xw7qwq/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md). Follow the [code of conduct](https://github.com/xw7qwq/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) when participating.

Feedback is handled according to impact and maintainer availability. There is no commercial support commitment, guaranteed response time, or online judge account appeal service. Contact third-party platforms through their official support channels.
